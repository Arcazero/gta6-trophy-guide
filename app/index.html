    Roadmap to the platinum

    About 200 hours at difficulty 4/10, in one playthrough — PowerPyx's estimate. There is no mission replay, so keep manual saves at the start and end of each chapter, and do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation while you can. Everything after the story can be finished in free-roam.

      Missable trophies

      PowerPyx and GTABase both report only one trophy that may be missable. Every other trophy can be earned after the story in free-roam — including all character questlines, which stay available after the credits.
